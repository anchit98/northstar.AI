#### Dr Pepper passed Pepsi as America's #2 soda in 2023. First time since 1985. And the chain of events behind it started in an orthodontist's office in St. George, Utah. Around 2010, patients from that office kept walking into a local drive-thru called Swig and ordering Dr Pepper with coconut cream. Within a month they'd nicknamed it a "dirty Dr Pepper." The founder trademarked "dirty soda" and rebuilt her whole menu around the idea. Utah was the perfect incubator. Half the state belongs to a church whose health code rules out coffee, tea, and alcohol. Customized soda became the local craft cocktail, the drink you socialize around. Swig says 95-97% of its orders now have something added to them. Then Olivia Rodrigo posted a Swig cup in December 2021 and the trend escaped Utah. Sonic added coconut cream and lime. Jack in the Box built dirty sodas around Dr Pepper. McDonald's put a Dirty Dr Pepper on the menu at nearly 14,000 locations this May. Menu penetration sits at just 2% but grows 42% a year. Dr Pepper rode the wave harder than anyone. Creamy Coconut, a bottled tribute to Swig's original drink, became Keurig Dr Pepper's most successful limited-time soda ever. Pepsi launched Wild Cherry & Cream and Dirty Mountain Dew this year, two years behind the trend that cost it the #2 spot it held for decades. A milkshake with chocolate cake blended in is the logical endpoint. The line between soda and dessert already collapsed. Pepsi spent a century fighting Coke. Second place went to a drink invented by orthodontist patients who couldn't order a cocktail.
